Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026.

ObjectLog is an app for keeping track of a field professional's clients, job sites, work, photos, and reports. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Who is responsible for the app

Developer: Maksim Grebenozhko.

Privacy and support contact: maksgr.soft@yandex.com.

Data stored on the device

ObjectLog works without registration or an account. Client, job site, work, photo, report, professional profile, and app settings data is stored locally on the user's device.

ObjectLog does not upload this data to its own server. The user decides what to export or send through the iOS share sheet, such as a PDF report, a business card image, or a contact.

Access to device features

The app may request access only for actions selected by the user:

  • Photos and Camera — to add photos to work records or reports;
  • Contacts — to select a contact or open a saved contact;
  • Location — to determine a job site's address from the current location.

Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. ObjectLog does not send location data to its analytics system. Reverse geocoding is performed by Apple system services and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Address suggestion service

When the user enters an address and uses suggestions, the entered text is sent to DaData to find matching addresses. Photos, contacts, and work record contents are not sent to this service.

DaData processes the request under its privacy policy.

An address can be entered manually without selecting a suggestion.

Analytics and diagnostics

ObjectLog uses AppMetrica to understand how the app is used, measure performance, and diagnose errors. The service may process technical and diagnostic data, including:

  • events describing the use of app features;
  • app version, device model, iOS version, language, and region;
  • technical app and device identifiers;
  • performance measurements;
  • crash and error information;
  • technical network data, including the IP address received by the service during a connection.

ObjectLog does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not request permission for advertising tracking. Location collection is disabled in AppMetrica.

AppMetrica processes data under its privacy documents.

Sharing with third parties

Data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • when the user explicitly exports or sends data through system features;
  • with AppMetrica for analytics, performance measurement, and diagnostics;
  • with DaData to provide suggestions for an address entered by the user;
  • with Apple when iOS system features are used, including geocoding, Photos, Camera, Contacts, and the share sheet.

The developer does not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it in the app or removes the app. AppMetrica, DaData, and Apple determine the retention periods for data they process under their respective policies.

Because ObjectLog does not create a server account or store a user database on its own server, local data is deleted on the device. Questions about analytics data can be sent to the support address.

Security

Network requests use secure connections. However, no method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Children

ObjectLog is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact maksgr.soft@yandex.com.
